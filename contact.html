Before you write

Quick answers

The five questions buyers ask us most. If yours isn't here, the full list is on our FAQ page.

What is your MOQ?
Our MOQ starts from 500 pcs per model, across all product lines. There is no lower quantity tier for standard production orders — for smaller runs we offer sample orders instead.
How fast can I get samples?
Sampling takes 7-14 days after sample fees and technical files are confirmed. For first-time cooperation the sample fee is waived — you only cover the courier cost. Every inquiry also receives a proposal with 3D rendering and quotation within 24 hours.
Do you support OEM and ODM?
Both. OEM covers your logo, packaging, colours and finishes on our validated platforms. ODM starts from concept — supported by our core strategic partner Bole Design (20 years, 500+ design awards) — and continues through our own production.
Which certifications can your products carry?
Our quality systems are ISO 9001 and ISO 14001, with amfori BSCI social-compliance audit. Products can carry CE, RoHS, FCC, PSE and UL per model and destination market. Battery products ship with UN38.3 / MSDS documentation. Exact certificates per SKU are confirmed during quotation.
What are your payment terms?
We accept T/T (30% deposit, 70% balance before shipment) and letters of credit (L/C). Exact terms appear on the proforma invoice for each order.
