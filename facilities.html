Facilities & Certifications

Where your products are actually made.

A manufacturing network built over 12+ years — components and finished products, in China and Myanmar, under audited quality, environmental and social-compliance systems. Visits and third-party audits are welcome.

Key Facts

  • Panyu, Guangzhou (China): components plant covering more than 10,000 sqm with 600+ employees — power inductors, switching power transformers, common chokes, antennas, wireless-charging coils, SMT assembly and EMI line filters, serving automotive and medical applications; R&D headed by a chief engineer with decades of Singapore electronics-industry experience.
  • Shenzhen (China): 3,000 sqm finished-product facility for body-care devices — assembly, aging tests and outbound QC for heating, air-pressure massage and NIR platforms.
  • Yangon (Myanmar): production plant with 5,000+ pcs/day capacity for labor-intensive finished-goods assembly.
  • Systems & certificates: ISO 9001 quality management, ISO 14001 environmental management, amfori BSCI social compliance; UL-certified plant; RoHS-compliant and Sony Green Partner environmental standards since 2003; products carry CE / ROHS / FCC / PSE / UL per model and market.
  • Audits: customer visits and third-party audits (SGS, TÜV, BSCI and others) are welcome at all plants.
