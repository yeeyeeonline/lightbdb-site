Process

How an engagement runs

01 Product Strategy

Market insight, user research and category positioning — deciding what to build and why it will win.

02 Category Innovation

Concept, CMF and form-factor innovation to create a defensible product, not another me-too SKU.

03 Engineering Handover

DFM-ready industrial design, structures and electronics handed to our production engineering team.

04 Mass Production

Sampling in 7-14 days, ISO 9001 quality system, certification support, and delivery from Shenzhen or Yangon.
