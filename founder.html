The Founder · 创始人

A bridge between Eastern making and Western brands.

Yimeng Shao (邵艺萌, Simon Shao) — founder of LightBDB. His conviction: become a bridge where Eastern product craft and Western brand culture meet — 胸怀成为东西方产品贸易与文化圆融的桥梁.

Who he is, in checkable facts

  • Founder: Yimeng Shao (邵艺萌 / Simon Shao), founder of LIGHT BRAND DESIGN BUSINESS (SHENZHEN) CO., LTD. — Unified Social Credit Code 91440300358219445L, founded 2015-09-22, Shenzhen.
  • Manufacturing insider: previously served as general manager of the Panyu production facility (Fuli Electronics / Guangzhou Fuyin Electronics, USCC 91440101578010598H) — the same site that manufactures LightBDB platforms today.
  • Named inventor: US design patent D1,024,003 S (Bluetooth speaker), published 2024-04 — patents.justia.com/patent/D1024003.
  • Design recognition: shortlisted by name, Second Chinese Design Award (product category), for Glow of Sunrise — and the same lamp later won the 2023 Canton Fair Design Innovation Award (CF Award, Bronze) with our manufacturing partner as awardee of record.
  • In the press: Shanghai Youth Daily designer interview (2016) · Jiemian News on the Tree of Light · Australian Giftguide at the Hong Kong Mega Show (2017) · TechAcute on Maglamp (2020).
From the factory floor

He managed the factory before he founded the brand

Before founding LightBDB in 2015, Simon served as general manager of the Panyu plant — 10,000+ sqm, 600+ people, making transformers, inductors and wireless-charging coils for automotive- and medical-grade customers. That decade left him with a habit that still runs this company: never claim what cannot be shown. It is why this site publishes real audit systems (ISO 9001 · ISO 14001 · BSCI · IATF 16949 heritage · Sony Green Partner), why factory photographs here are real photographs, and why every quotation comes with certificate copies and audit references on request.

The designer's eye

A patent holder who still sketches

Simon is a named inventor on US design patent D1,024,003 S, and was shortlisted by name for the Second Chinese Design Award. LightBDB's design capability runs from Hangzhou and Hamburg together with core strategic partner Bole Design — 500+ design awards across the partnership. His own product line, LIGHT&MIND (Tree of Light, Glow of Sunrise, companion audio and aroma pieces), is where the aesthetics get tested first: if a lamp would not sit on his own desk, it does not become your platform.

The bridge

What "East meets West" means in practice

胸怀成为东西方产品贸易与文化圆融的桥梁 — the sentence he built the company on.

Eastern making, honestly shown

Three production sites (Panyu, Shenzhen, Yangon) run on audited systems. What you see on this site is what auditors see on site — no staged showrooms, no borrowed certificates.

Western brands, seriously served

Private-label launches in 4–8 weeks, MOQ from 100 (Light & Mood platforms) to 500 (Body care platforms) depending on the platform, 48-hour quotation, 500-day warranty, FOB Shenzhen. The bridge only works if both sides can walk it.

Culture in the product

中文版

东方造物与西方品牌之间的桥

邵艺萌（Simon Shao），轻无品牌设计商业（深圳）有限公司创始人（统一社会信用代码 91440300358219445L，2015 年 9 月 22 日成立）。创业之前，他是番禺工厂总经理——一万平米、六百人、车规与医疗级客户的元件厂。那十年给他留下一个规矩：凡是不能拿出来给人看的，就绝不说。所以今天这个网站上：工厂照片是实拍，体系认证可查验，报价附证书副本。

他也是美国外观专利 D1,024,003 S 的署名发明人，曾以个人名义入围第二届中国设计奖。设计能力由杭州与汉堡两地、携手核心战略伙伴博乐设计共同交付。自有品牌 LIGHT&MIND 是他的审美试验田——过不了自己书桌的东西，不会变成客户的平台。

他的心愿一句话：成为东西方产品贸易与文化圆融的桥梁。桥这一头，是被诚实展示的东方制造；桥那一头，是被认真服务的西方品牌——4–8 周贴牌上市、MOQ 按平台 100–500 起、48 小时报价、500 天质保、FOB 深圳。桥两头都能走，桥才算桥。
