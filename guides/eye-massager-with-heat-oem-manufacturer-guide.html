What certifications does an eye massager with heat need for US, EU, and JP markets?

For US, EU, and JP markets, your eye massager with heat will need electrical safety testing, and your factory should hold ISO 9001 and BSCI as baseline credentials.

LIGHT BDB holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI (amfori), UL registered factory status, and Sony Green Partner certification. These are the credentials that matter for a wellness device. The UL registered factory status is particularly valuable for US market entry, as it indicates the factory's processes have been audited against UL standards. For the EU, you will need CE marking, which is typically arranged through the factory or a third-party testing lab. For Japan, PSE certification is required for electrical products, and your OEM partner should be familiar with the process.

It is critical to understand what your factory is not certified for. LIGHT BDB does not hold ISO 13485 and no product is FDA approved. This is a wellness device, not a medical device. If a competitor claims FDA approval for an eye massager, that is a red flag. The FDA does not "approve" wellness devices in the same way it clears medical devices. A reputable OEM will be transparent about this distinction.

Certification checklist for your eye massager with heat

  • Factory-level: ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI (amfori), UL registered factory.
  • Product-level for US: UL or ETL listing for electrical safety.
  • Product-level for EU: CE marking (LVD + EMC directives).
  • Product-level for JP: PSE certification for electrical appliances.
  • Battery: UN38.3 transport certification if your massager uses a lithium-ion battery.

How do payment terms and shipping work with an eye massager OEM factory?

Expect a T/T 30/70 payment structure, meaning 30% deposit to start production and 70% balance before shipment, with FOB Shenzhen as the standard incoterm.

LIGHT BDB works on T/T 30/70 or L/C at sight. The 30% deposit secures your production slot and covers component procurement. The 70% balance is due before the goods leave the factory. This is standard practice in the industry, and it protects both parties. You are not paying the full amount upfront, and the factory is not shipping without payment.

For shipping, LIGHT BDB works with your nominated forwarder. This is a significant advantage for first-time buyers because it means you control the logistics and can compare freight quotes. FOB Shenzhen means the factory is responsible for getting the goods to the port and loaded, and your forwarder takes over from there. If you do not have a forwarder yet, your OEM partner can recommend one, but you should get quotes from at least two or three to ensure competitive pricing.

Payment and shipping checklist

  • Confirm the deposit percentage (30% is standard with LIGHT BDB).
  • Clarify whether the balance is due before or after inspection.
  • Use a third-party inspection service for your first order to verify quality before the balance payment.
  • Book your freight forwarder early, as container space can be tight during peak seasons.

How do I choose between an eye massager with heat and a regular eye massager for my brand?

Choose a heated model if your target customer values relaxation and stress relief, as heat is the most requested feature in this category.

Heat adds a therapeutic element that a regular eye massager without heat cannot replicate. It helps with eye strain and provides a spa-like experience at home. The trade-off is that a heated model requires more complex components, which increases the unit cost and the certification requirements. A regular eye massager with only vibration and air pressure is simpler and cheaper, but it competes in a more crowded market.

For most brands, the heated version is the better choice because it justifies a higher retail price and differentiates your product. The additional cost at the factory level is typically $3 to $8 per unit, depending on the heating element quality and battery size. You can easily recover this cost with a $10 to $20 higher retail price point.
