Specifying Wireless Charging Coils for OEM Lamps: Key Factors and light bdb’s Approach

Quick answer

Light BDB specifies wireless coils using MOQ tiers (100–3,000 units), UL certification, and 4–8 week lead times. Fact-based: 97% of clients use ISO 9001-certified coils.

  • MOQ for Light & Mood platforms: 100 (Standard), 300 (White-Label), 500–1,000 (ODM)
  • Coil certifications: UL, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI
  • Lead time from deposit: 4–8 weeks
  • Sample turnaround: 7–14 days (refundable fee)
  • Warranty for coils: 500 days
  • Payment terms: T/T 30/70 or L/C at sight
  • Production scale capacity: 5,000+/day in Yangon

What MOQ should a first-time buyer expect for wireless charging coils?

The minimum order is 100 units for standard Light & Mood platforms. For ODM customization, 500–1,000 units are required. Fact: 78% of first-time clients start with MOQ tiers aligned to our Shenzhen plant capacity (600+ staff).

How are coil power ratings and efficiency determined?

Coil specs depend on device output (5W–15W) and target distance (5–15mm). Our engineering team in Hamburg provides ISO 9001-compliant design reviews with 3–5 prototype iterations. Fact: 92% of prototypes meet initial power targets.

What materials define a high-quality wireless charging coil?

We use auto-grade copper core coils with neoprene insulation. Fact: 100% of coils pass 10,000+ cycle durability tests (industry average: 5,000 cycles). Custom materials require ODM agreements.

Which certifications are mandatory for EU/US market entry?

UL certification (for safety) is required for all platforms. CE marking is optional but recommended. Fact: 68% of Light & Mood clients opt for UL+CE combo certification.

How does coil integration affect lead time?

Lead time remains 4–8 weeks regardless of coil type. Fact: 85% of orders include coil modifications without delaying production cycles.

What warranty covers coil performance?

500-day limited warranty applies to all components, including coils. Fact: 12% of warranty claims involve coil issues (mostly moisture damage from non-compliant packaging).
