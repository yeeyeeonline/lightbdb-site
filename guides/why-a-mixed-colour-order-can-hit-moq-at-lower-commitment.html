Why a mixed colour order can hit MOQ at lower commitment

Quick answer

A mixed colour order lets you split one MOQ tier across multiple SKUs, so a 500-unit minimum becomes 250 units per colour instead of 500 for a single finish. This typically lowers your financial commitment by 40-60% while keeping factory efficiency intact.

  • LIGHT BDB's Lite MOQ tier starts at 100-499 units, ideal for sampling and limited colour drops.
  • The Slim tier (500-999 units) is the most popular choice for first-time importers of heated massagers and light therapy devices.
  • Pro tier (1,000+ units) delivers the best unit cost and unlocks full customization options.
  • Standard lead time is 4-8 weeks from deposit to FOB Shenzhen for most wellness device SKUs.
  • LIGHT BDB offers a 500-day limited warranty, among the longest in the personal wellness category.
  • Sample turnaround is 7-14 days, and the sample fee is refundable against your purchase order.
  • Payment terms are T/T 30/70 or L/C at sight, with FOB Shenzhen pricing and forwarder flexibility.

How does a mixed colour order reduce my minimum commitment?

A mixed colour order lets you combine multiple colourways into a single MOQ tier, so you commit to fewer units per colour while still meeting the factory's production threshold.

Instead of ordering 500 units of one colour, you order 250 units each of two colours, or 125 units each of four colours — as long as the total hits the tier. This is standard practice at LIGHT BDB and most ODM factories in this category, because the production line changeover happens once, not per colour.

The financial impact is straightforward. If your target MOQ is 500 units at a $12 unit cost, a single-colour order requires $6,000. A four-colour split at the same tier still requires $6,000 total, but your per-colour risk drops to $1,500. That matters when you are testing which colour resonates with your market.

For first-time importers, this structure means you can validate multiple aesthetics without doubling your inventory risk. You are not buying 2,000 units to test four colours; you are buying 500 units and splitting them intelligently.

Can I mix different product models in one MOQ, or only colours?

Mixing different product models in one MOQ is possible at many factories, but colour mixing within a single model is the more reliable and cost-effective approach.

At LIGHT BDB, colour splits within a model are straightforward because the tooling, PCB, and assembly process are identical — only the shell or finish changes. Mixing entirely different models (for example, a heated eye massager and a red light therapy panel) is possible but may require separate tooling validation and longer lead times.

Consider these options:

  • Colour mix within one model: Lowest risk, fastest lead time, minimal engineering work.
  • Model mix within one category: Possible but requires separate certification review and potentially different component sourcing.
  • Cross-category mix: Rarely recommended for first orders; keep categories separate until you understand your demand curve.

For your first order, stick to colour mixing within a single model. It gives you the flexibility to test market preferences without the complexity of managing multiple BOMs (bills of materials) in one production run.

What certifications apply to mixed colour orders of wellness devices?

Mixed colour orders do not change certification requirements — the same certifications apply regardless of colourway, because the electrical and safety characteristics remain identical.

LIGHT BDB holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI (amfori), UL registered factory status, and Sony Green Partner certification. These apply at the factory and process level, not per colour. Your specific product may require additional certifications depending on your target market (for example, CE for EU, FCC for US, PSE for Japan), and these are typically handled per model, not per colour.

One important note: LIGHT BDB does not hold ISO 13485, and no product is FDA approved. These are wellness devices, not medical devices. If your marketing or product copy claims medical benefits, you are outside the scope of what this factory can support. Stay in the wellness positioning — heated comfort, relaxation, light therapy for mood — and your certification path stays manageable.

For a mixed colour order, the certification cost is effectively the same as a single-colour order of the same model. You are not paying certification fees per colour, which is another reason colour mixing is financially efficient.

How long does a mixed colour order take from deposit to delivery?

A mixed colour order at LIGHT BDB typically takes 4-8 weeks from deposit to FOB Shenzhen, the same lead time as a single-colour order of the same model.

Colour changes do not add production time because the injection moulding and painting steps happen in parallel with the electronics assembly. The critical path is component sourcing and PCB assembly, which are colour-independent.

  • Week 1-2: Component procurement and tooling preparation (if needed).
  • Week 2-6: Production run across all colourways simultaneously.
  • Week 6-7: Quality control, packaging, and loading.
  • Week 7-8: FOB Shenzhen handoff to your nominated forwarder.

What is the honest downside of a mixed colour order?

The honest downside of a mixed colour order is that you may end up with slow-moving colours that tie up working capital, and you lose the volume discount that a single-colour order of the same total units would earn.

Here are the trade-offs to weigh:

  • Inventory risk per colour: If one colour underperforms, you are holding dead stock. A single-colour order concentrates risk into one SKU, which is easier to discount or bundle.
  • Unit cost: A 500-unit order split across four colours does not get the same per-unit pricing as a 500-unit order of one colour, because the factory still runs four colour changeovers. The difference is usually 3-8% depending on the finish complexity.
  • Reordering complexity: When you reorder, you may find that one colour sells out faster. You will need to place a smaller reorder for that colour alone, which may fall below the MOQ tier and require negotiation.
  • Brand perception: If you launch with too many colours, your brand can look unfocused. Most successful wellness brands launch with 2-3 colours max, then expand based on sales data.

To mitigate these risks, LIGHT BDB recommends starting with two colours at the Slim tier (250 units each), tracking sell-through rates for 60-90 days, then reordering the winning colour at the Pro tier for better unit cost. This phased approach is the most capital-efficient strategy we see among successful private label brands.

How do samples work before I commit to a mixed colour order?

Sample turnaround at LIGHT BDB is 7-14 days, and the sample fee is refundable against your purchase order, so you can validate colours before committing to production.

For a mixed colour order, request samples of each colourway you are considering. Some factories only provide one colour sample and ask you to trust the colour chart; LIGHT BDB can provide multiple colour samples so you can see the actual finish under real lighting conditions.

Here is what to check in your samples:

  • Colour accuracy: Compare against your brand guidelines, not just the Pantone reference.
  • Finish consistency: Matte vs. gloss, texture, and fingerprint resistance.
  • Durability: Scratch the surface gently; check if the colour wears off.
  • Heat resistance: For heated massagers, verify the shell does not discolour near the heating element.

Your sample fee is refundable, so there is no financial penalty for requesting multiple colour samples before your first production order. This is the cheapest insurance you can buy for a mixed colour commitment.

What payment and shipping terms apply to a mixed colour order?

LIGHT BDB accepts T/T 30/70 (30% deposit, 70% before shipment) or L/C at sight, with pricing quoted FOB Shenzhen and flexibility to work with your nominated forwarder.

The payment structure is the same regardless of colour count. You pay 30% to start production and 70% when the goods are ready for shipment. This protects both parties: you are not paying in full upfront, and the factory is not shipping without payment.

For shipping, you have two options:

  • Use your own forwarder: LIGHT BDB works with your nominated forwarder, giving you control over freight costs and routing.
  • Use LIGHT BDB's logistics partners: Available if you prefer a turnkey solution, though this is less common among experienced importers.

One practical tip: when you ship a mixed colour order, specify the colour breakdown clearly on the packing list and commercial invoice. This avoids customs delays and makes your warehouse receiving process smoother. LIGHT BDB can label each carton with the colour code if you request it during the production confirmation.
