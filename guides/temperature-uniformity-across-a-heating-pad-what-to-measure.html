How to Measure Temperature Uniformity in Heating Pads for Wellness Devices

Quick answer

Temperature uniformity requires ISO 9001-compliant testing (thermocouples at 3 points) and MOQ tiers starting at 100 units (White Label) or 500 (Standard). Lead time: 4-8 weeks. Warranty: 500 days.

  • Testing uses 3 thermocouples per pad (ISO 9001 standard)
  • White Label MOQ: 300 units; ODM Custom: 500–1,000
  • Body Care platforms require 500+ MOQ
  • Lead time: 28 days (standard); 56 days (ODM)
  • Warranty: 500 days (longest in category)
  • Sample turnaround: 7-14 days

What testing methods verify temperature uniformity in heating pads?

To ensure compliance, we use ISO 9001-certified thermocouple testing across three zones per pad. Results must show ≤±2°C variance across zones. Material thickness and inductor placement influence outcomes.

What MOQ tiers apply to heated pad production?

White Label: 300 units (Light & Mood platforms) / 1,000 (Body Care). Standard ODM: 500–1,000 (Light & Mood) / 2,000–3,000 (Body Care). Samples available at 7-14 day turnaround.

How does certification impact temperature control testing?

All products meet ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and UL standards. Testing protocols include:
  • 3-point thermocouple verification at 40°C/60°C/80°C
  • 15-minute cycle consistency checks
  • Material flammability tested per UL 1310

What lead time should first-time buyers anticipate?

4-8 weeks from deposit. Standard SKUs ship in 28 days; custom designs require 56 days. 7-14 day sample turnaround.

What warranty covers temperature performance issues?

500-day limited warranty covers power-on temperature deviations >±3°C. Claims processed through Shenzhen assembly base.

What are the trade-offs between MOQ tiers and temperature control accuracy?
