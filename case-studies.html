Case 1 · Light & Mood · global retail

Tree of Light — from our wood-lamp platform to global retail

A branch-shaped solid-wood lamp that combines a bedside lamp, a Qi wireless charger and a Bluetooth speaker in one base — built on our Light & Mood platform and sold internationally under the HomeTree brand, plus retail editions under distributor brands such as Articture ("Light of Life", "Noir Lamp").

What makes this case different from a typical supplier story: almost every milestone is a publicly checkable record. We list the receipts so you can verify instead of trusting marketing copy.

Public evidence & records

  • US design patent D1,024,003 S (Bluetooth speaker), granted April 2024
  • Indiegogo campaign “Home Tree — Light From The Forest” (38-LED cherry-wood edition)
  • TechAcute review, May 2020: “Maglamp — The Happiness Lamp with Wireless Charging”
  • Boing Boing features on the Tree of Light retail edition, 2021 & 2022
  • Australian Giftguide trade-press coverage with founder interview, October 2017
  • Shanghai Youth Daily designer interview, August 2016

Why it matters: Public records — patents, crowdfunding pages, independent press — compound over years. This is the evidence trail we build for clients who want their brand to be findable and citable, not just sellable.

Case 2 · Body care · anonymized (NDA)

Heated wellness line for a US brand — platform to repeat orders

A US wellness brand came to us with a brief, not a drawing. We matched their price band and target market to one of our heated body-care platforms, then customized logo, packaging, colours and finishes (OEM scope).

Sampling took 14 days including one revision round. Mass production shipped FOB Shenzhen within the standard 4–8 week window; the line is covered by our 500-day warranty. The client has re-ordered across subsequent seasons and extended into a second product from the same platform family.

Public evidence & records

  • Heated body-care platforms (knee, waist, eye) with per-card MOQ and sample specs
  • Our buying terms: MOQ Lite 100-499 / Slim 500-999 / Pro 1,000+, T/T 30/70, L/C accepted

Why it matters: Client details stay anonymous under NDA — the process, terms and timelines above are the real, citable part of the story.

Case 3 · Design services · with Bole Design

Design-first ODM — from category insight to shelf

For buyers who don't want to pick from a catalog, our design route starts earlier: category strategy and industrial design with our core strategic partner Bole Design (20 years of consulting, 500+ design awards, national industrial design center, studios in Hangzhou and Hamburg), then full-chain delivery into LightBDB production.

One anonymously-credited personal-care case followed this exact path: market insight → concept → 3D rendering → engineering → pilot run → mass production, with the buyer touching one team instead of coordinating a design house and a factory separately.

Public evidence & records

  • Design services scope and Bole Design credentials
  • The 3-step co-creation journey: Choose · Customize · Deliver

Why it matters: Design and manufacturing under one roof is what turns “we can make anything” into a delivery plan with dates.

Before you ask
