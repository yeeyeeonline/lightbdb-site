How this SKU is made

The Heated Knee Wrap starts with components from our Panyu plant — 10,000+ sqm with 600+ staff — where auto-grade inductors, transformers, wireless charging coils, and SMT assemblies are produced. Finished-product assembly happens on our 3,000 sqm Shenzhen line, with our Yangon overflow line adding 5,000+ pcs/day capacity when demand peaks. Design support comes from our Hangzhou and Hamburg studios, and the factory operates under ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI, UL registered factory, and Sony Green Partner programs. OEM / ODM questions

What are the MOQ tiers for the Heated Knee Wrap?

Lite is 100–499 units, Slim is 500–999 units (the most popular tier), and Pro is 1,000+ units. The Pro tier gives you the best unit cost and full customization.

How does the sample process work?

Sample turnaround is 7 days, and the sample fee is refundable against your first PO. That lets you test fit, warmth, and branding before committing.

What is the production lead time?

4–8 weeks from deposit to FOB Shenzhen for most SKUs. Your nominated forwarder can pick up at our Shenzhen plant.

What warranty can I offer my customers?

A 500-day limited warranty — one of the longest in the category. It's a strong selling point for DTC brands.

What are your trade terms?

T/T 30/70 (30% deposit, 70% before shipment) or L/C at sight. All pricing is FOB Shenzhen, and we work with your nominated forwarder.

What certifications does the factory hold?

ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI (amfori), UL registered factory, and Sony Green Partner. Tell us your target market at RFQ and we'll discuss what documentation you need.
