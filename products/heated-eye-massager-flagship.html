How this SKU is made

The flagship eye massager starts in our Panyu component plant — 10,000+ sqm with 600+ staff — where auto-grade inductors, transformers, and wireless charging coils are produced and SMT lines place the electronics. Finished-product assembly happens at our Shenzhen plant (3,000 sqm), with our Yangon line (5,000+ pcs/day) handling overflow when your order peaks. Design support comes from our Hangzhou and Hamburg studios, so the final product looks as considered as it feels. Request a quote to see how this build fits your brand.
