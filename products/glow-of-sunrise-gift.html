How this SKU is made

Every Glow of Sunrise · Gift unit starts at our Panyu component plant, where 600+ staff manufacture the auto-grade inductors, transformers, and wireless charging coils that power the device. Finished-product assembly happens in our 3,000 sqm Shenzhen plant, and when your order peaks, our Yangon line adds 5,000+ pcs/day overflow capacity. Design work is split between our Hangzhou and Hamburg studios, giving you Asian manufacturing efficiency with European design polish. OEM / ODM questions

What is the minimum order quantity for Glow of Sunrise · Gift?

Three tiers: Lite (100–499 units) to test the market, Slim (500–999 units) — our most popular — and Pro (1,000+ units) for the best unit cost and full customization. Your RFQ will tell you which tier fits your budget.

How long does it take to get my order?

4–8 weeks from deposit to FOB Shenzhen for most SKUs. That window covers component production, assembly, and packaging. Request a quote to get a date-specific schedule.

How does the sample process work?

7-day sample turnaround. The sample fee is refundable against your PO, so the only real cost is the week of waiting. Ask for samples in each finish you're considering.

What warranty can I offer my customers?

500 days limited warranty — one of the longest in the category. It's a strong gift-channel selling point because the recipient knows the product is built to last.

What are your payment and shipping terms?

T/T 30/70 (30% deposit, 70% before shipment) or L/C at sight. We ship FOB Shenzhen and work with your nominated forwarder, so you keep control of logistics.

What certifications do you hold?

ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 for quality and environmental management, BSCI (amfori) for social compliance, UL registered factory status, and Sony Green Partner recognition. Your RFQ can request the documentation pack.
