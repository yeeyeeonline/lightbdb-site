How this SKU is made

Your fireplace starts at our Panyu component plant — 10,000+ sqm with 600+ staff — where auto-grade inductors, transformers, and wireless charging coils are produced and control boards go through SMT. Finished-product assembly happens at our Shenzhen plant (3,000 sqm), with a Yangon overflow line capable of 5,000+ pcs/day for peak-season volume. Design centers in Hangzhou and Hamburg support the industrial design and flame-effect tuning. The factory holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI (amfori), UL registration, and Sony Green Partner status. Ask for a factory walkthrough video when you submit your RFQ at https://www.lightbdb.com/rfq.

OEM / ODM questions

What are the MOQ tiers for a Real Fireplace?

Lite: 100–499 units. Slim: 500–999 units — the most popular tier for brand owners testing a new SKU. Pro: 1,000+ units for the best unit cost and full customization. See the RFQ page to match a tier to your launch plan.

What is the lead time?

4–8 weeks from deposit to FOB Shenzhen for most SKUs. Custom tooling or a new form factor in the Pro tier can extend that window; the RFQ page lets you note your target ship date.

How does the sample process work?

7-day sample turnaround. The sample fee is refundable against your first PO, so you can validate flame effect, heat output, and finish before committing. Request a sample on the RFQ page.

What warranty do you offer?

500 days limited warranty — one of the longest in the category. It covers your end customers and reduces your after-sales risk. Ask about warranty terms for your market on the RFQ page.

What are your trade terms?

T/T 30/70 (30% deposit, 70% before shipment) or L/C at sight. All pricing is FOB Shenzhen, and we work with your nominated forwarder. Get current terms on the RFQ page.

What factory certifications does LIGHT BDB hold?

ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI (amfori), UL registered factory, and Sony Green Partner. These are factory-level credentials; product-level certification for your target market is coordinated during the project. Note your compliance needs on the RFQ page.
