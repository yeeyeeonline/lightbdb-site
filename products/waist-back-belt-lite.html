How this SKU is made

Every Waist & Back Belt · Lite starts with components from our Panyu plant — a 10,000+ sqm facility with 600+ staff producing auto-grade inductors, transformers, wireless charging coils, and SMT assemblies. Finished belts are assembled and quality-checked at our 3,000 sqm Shenzhen plant, with our Yangon overflow line (5,000+ pcs/day capacity) absorbing peak-season volume. Design input comes from our Hangzhou and Hamburg studios, so you get European-influenced design with Chinese manufacturing economics. OEM / ODM questions

What is the minimum order quantity for the Lite tier?

The Lite tier starts at 100–499 units. If you need more, the Slim tier (500–999) is our most popular, and the Pro tier (1,000+) delivers the best unit cost and full customization. Share your volume on the RFQ page and we'll recommend the right tier.

How fast can I get a sample?

Sample turnaround is 7 days from spec confirmation. The sample fee is refundable against your first PO, so the only real cost is time. Request your sample through the RFQ page.

What is the production lead time?

Most SKUs ship 4–8 weeks from deposit to FOB Shenzhen. Customization depth and order tier affect where you land in that window. Lock in your production slot by submitting an RFQ.

What warranty do you offer?

Every unit carries a 500-day limited warranty — one of the longest in the heated wellness category. That gives your US customers confidence and reduces your after-sales burden. Ask about warranty terms on the RFQ page.

What are your payment and shipping terms?

We work on T/T 30/70 (30% deposit, 70% before shipment) and accept L/C at sight. All pricing is FOB Shenzhen, and we're happy to work with your nominated forwarder. Get the full terms on the RFQ page.

Can I use my own freight forwarder?

Yes. We ship FOB Shenzhen and routinely coordinate with buyer-nominated forwarders. Include your forwarder's details on the RFQ page and we'll align the handoff.
