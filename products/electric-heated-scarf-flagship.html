How this SKU is made

LIGHT BRAND DESIGN BUSINESS (SHENZHEN) CO., LTD. builds the flagship heated scarf across three facilities. The Panyu component plant (10,000+ sqm, 600+ staff) produces auto-grade inductors, transformers, and wireless charging coils, with SMT assembly for the control boards. Finished-product assembly happens at our Shenzhen plant (3,000 sqm), and a Yangon overflow line (5,000+ pcs/day) covers peak-season volume. Design support comes from our Hangzhou and Hamburg design centers, and the factory operates under ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI (amfori), UL-registered factory status, and Sony Green Partner certification.
