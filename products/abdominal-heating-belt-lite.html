OEM / ODM questions

What is the MOQ for the Lite tier?

The Lite tier starts at 100–499 units. If you need more, the Slim tier (500–999 units) is our most popular, and the Pro tier (1,000+ units) gives the best unit cost and full customization.

What is the lead time?

Most SKUs ship 4–8 weeks from deposit to FOB Shenzhen. Customization depth and order size can shift where you land in that range.

How does the sample process work?

We turn samples around in 7 days, and the sample fee is refundable against your PO. That means you can validate the product before committing to bulk.

What warranty do you offer?

Every unit carries a 500-day limited warranty — one of the longest in the category. That gives your end customers confidence and reduces your after-sales risk.

What are your trade terms?

We work on T/T 30/70 (30% deposit, 70% before shipment) and accept L/C at sight. Shipments are FOB Shenzhen, and we'll coordinate with your nominated forwarder.

What certifications does the factory hold?

LIGHT BDB is ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certified, BSCI (amfori) audited, a UL registered factory, and a Sony Green Partner. These are factory-level credentials you can verify during qualification.
