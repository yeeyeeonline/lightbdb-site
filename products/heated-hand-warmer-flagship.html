OEM / ODM questions

Lite: 100–499 units for testing a SKU. Slim: 500–999 units — our most popular tier for brand launches. Pro: 1,000+ units for the best unit cost and full customization. Not sure which fits? State your target volume on the RFQ page and we'll recommend a tier.

What is your lead time?

4–8 weeks from deposit to FOB Shenzhen for most SKUs. Complex ODM programs may take longer; we confirm a schedule at RFQ.

How does the sample process work?

7-day sample turnaround. The sample fee is refundable against your PO, so the only real cost is the week of waiting. Request a sample through the RFQ page.

What warranty can we offer our customers?

500 days limited warranty — one of the longest in the category. It's a meaningful differentiator on your product page and a signal of build quality.

What are your trade terms?

T/T 30/70 (30% deposit, 70% before shipment) or L/C at sight. All pricing is FOB Shenzhen, and we work with your nominated forwarder. See the RFQ page for pricing and terms.

What certifications does the factory hold?

ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI (amfori), UL registered factory, and Sony Green Partner. These are factory-level credentials; product-level testing needs are discussed per program at RFQ.
