How this SKU is made

The Dance of Light starts in our Panyu component plant (10,000+ sqm, 600+ staff), where auto-grade inductors, transformers, wireless charging coils, and SMT boards are produced. Final assembly happens in our Shenzhen 3,000 sqm plant, with our Yangon line (5,000+ pcs/day) available as overflow for peak seasons. Design support comes from our Hangzhou and Hamburg studios, so your custom version is developed in-house from concept to production. Request a quote to reserve engineering time for your program.
