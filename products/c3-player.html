How this SKU is made

The C3 Player's electronics start at our Panyu component plant — 10,000+ sqm with 600+ staff producing auto-grade inductors, transformers, wireless charging coils, and SMT boards. Finished assembly happens at our Shenzhen plant (3,000 sqm), and our Yangon overflow line (5,000+ pcs/day) absorbs peak-season volume without slowing your launch. Industrial design and engineering support come from our Hangzhou and Hamburg design centers, giving you European-influenced design with Shenzhen manufacturing efficiency.
