How this SKU is made

The heated knee wrap starts at our Panyu component plant — 10,000+ sqm with 600+ staff — where auto-grade inductors, transformers, wireless charging coils, and SMT lines build the heating controller and coil sub-assemblies. Finished assembly happens in our Shenzhen plant (3,000 sqm), and our Yangon overflow line adds 5,000+ pcs/day capacity when your order peaks. Design is split between Hangzhou and Hamburg, so you get manufacturing economics and European design input in one SKU.
