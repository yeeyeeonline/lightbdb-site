  • Scale — tabletop, sideboard, or floor-standing presence

How this SKU is made

HomeTree starts as a design brief at our Hangzhou or Hamburg design center, where the silhouette is translated into production drawings. The electronic heart — LED drivers, inductors, transformers, and SMT assemblies — is built at our Panyu component plant, a 10,000+ sqm facility with 600+ staff that also produces auto-grade components. Finished-product assembly happens at our 3,000 sqm Shenzhen plant, with our Yangon overflow line (5,000+ pcs/day) absorbing volume surges when your launch exceeds plan. The factory holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI (amfori), UL registered factory status, and Sony Green Partner certification, so compliance documentation follows your order.
