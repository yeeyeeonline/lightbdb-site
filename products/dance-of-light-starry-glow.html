How this SKU is made

The Starry Glow edition starts at our Panyu component plant — 10,000+ sqm with 600+ staff producing the auto-grade inductors, transformers, wireless charging coils, and SMT assemblies used across LIGHT BDB products. Finished-product assembly happens at our Shenzhen plant (3,000 sqm), with a Yangon overflow line that adds 5,000+ pcs/day when your order peaks. Design work is split between our Hangzhou and Hamburg studios, so you get Asian manufacturing economics with European design input.
