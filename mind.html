Light, sound and scent — objects people keep.

Solid wood, natural salt rock, glass and marble. Our lifestyle line is built for brands that sell feeling, not specs: oriental aesthetics, stepless dimming, and laser-engraved customization on real materials.

Key Facts

  • What we make: solid-wood lamps (branch-shaped, sunrise light), premium speakers and TWS audio, wireless charging objects, natural salt-rock air devices, aroma diffusers and humidifiers (700 ml glass / stainless steel / marble), scented candles.
  • Design language: oriental aesthetics and real materials — solid wood, salt rock, glass, marble; stepless dimming on lighting platforms.
  • Customization: laser engraving, custom finishes, packaging and gift sets; ODM from existing platforms or OEM to your design.
  • Where: developed with our Hangzhou and Hamburg design resources; production in our Shenzhen and Yangon facilities.
  • Focus: four categories — lighting · audio · wireless charging · aroma. (Product line intentionally focused, not a general catalogue.)
