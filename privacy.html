Privacy Policy

How we handle your data.

Short version: we only use what you send us to answer your inquiry. No newsletters, no sharing, no advertising tracking.

Details

  • What we collect: only the fields you submit in our inquiry forms (name, work email, company, product interest, target market, message).
  • Where it is stored: securely in Singapore (Supabase), accessible only to the LightBDB team handling your inquiry.
  • How it is used: to respond to your inquiry and prepare your quotation. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • Newsletters: we do not send newsletters. You will only hear from us about your inquiry.
  • Cookies: minimal, performance-only. No advertising trackers. You can decline via the cookie banner.
  • Deletion: email and we will delete your data within 7 business days.
